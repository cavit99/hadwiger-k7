# Archived five-centre two-cut local campaign

**Status:** historical proof-development material; not part of the current
proof spine.  Archived on 11 August 2026 before the minimal-donor
connector-or-trace campaign.

This directory preserves one bounded local campaign around the unbounded
five-centre two-cut residue.

- `hc7_k7minus_five_centre_saturation_matrix.md` proves several palette
  identities but ends at an explicit failure to synchronize the shore
  colourings.
- `hc7_k7minus_five_centre_shared_pole_saturation.md`,
  `hc7_k7minus_five_centre_saturated_pair_fan_core.md`, and
  `hc7_k7minus_five_centre_core_tripod_funnel.md` refine that local
  configuration but return nonterminal rooted-placement obligations.
- `hc7_k7minus_five_centre_order11_rooted_k5_reduction.md` is superseded by
  the audited atom-slack theorem, which eliminates the order-eleven row
  outright and proves that the relevant all-rainbow shore has order at
  least fifteen.
- `hc7_k7minus_support_five_legless_tripod_elimination.md` and its adjacent
  GREEN internal audit prove an exact conditional configuration.  No
  exhaustive reduction to that configuration is known.  Its assumption
  that no centre is adjacent to either pole also prevents it from applying
  to the current arbitrary two-cut residue, where the audited reduction
  forces a centre--pole edge.
