\begin{abstract}
Let $K_7^{-}$ be the graph obtained from $K_7$ by deleting one edge.
We prove that every graph with no $K_7^{-}$ minor is six-colourable.
The main extremal result is that every $4$-bilight graph on $n\geq3$
vertices with at least $4n-2$ edges contains a $K_7^{-}$ minor.
The proof strengthens the rooted density construction of
Dvo\v r\'ak, Norin and Rahman: five prescribed root bags admit two
additional bags with at most one missing contact. A separator argument
then reduces the extremal theorem to six-connected graphs. The final
degree-seven case uses a finite verification of $232$ nine-vertex graphs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
All graphs in this paper are finite and simple. Write $K_7^{-}$ for
$K_7$ with one edge deleted. We prove the following statement, posed as
Conjecture~21 by Norin and Totschnig~\cite{NT2025}.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:colour}
Every graph with no $K_7^{-}$ minor is six-colourable.
\end{theorem}

Norin and Totschnig~\cite{NT2025} proved the corresponding result when
the excluded minor is $K_7$ with two incident edges deleted.
Dvo\v r\'ak, Norin and Rahman~\cite{DNR2026} proved it for $K_7$ with
two independent edges deleted. Theorem~\ref{thm:colour} strengthens
both conclusions. It does not settle Hadwiger's conjecture for $K_7$.

The proof uses the rooted density and separation framework
of Dvo\v r\'ak, Norin and Rahman. The critical-graph density bound,
also obtained independently in our earlier work~\cite[Corollary~3]{Erginsoy2026critical},
reduces Theorem~\ref{thm:colour} to an extremal problem. Our main result is
Theorem~\ref{thm:bilight}, for the class of $4$-bilight graphs defined
below. In particular, it proves their Conjecture~1.5:

\begin{corollary}\label{thm:five-connected}
Every five-connected graph on $n\geq6$ vertices with at least $4n-2$
edges contains a $K_7^{-}$ minor.
\end{corollary}

The rooted construction in~\cite[Theorem~2.9]{DNR2026} supplies two
additional bags alongside five prescribed root bags, but permits two
missing contacts among the eleven involving the additional bags.
Even if the root bags form a clique, this need not give $K_7^{-}$.
Our construction allows only one missing contact when the rooted
four-density is at least two. The main difficulty is that contracting
an edge can violate the separation conditions needed for induction.
We locate each such obstruction on a five-vertex boundary. A rooted-star
construction handles small configurations; an auxiliary clique then
allows Mader's atom theorem to select a smallest eligible fragment.
The resulting size bound contradicts a degree count inside the fragment.
The auxiliary clique is used only to select the fragment, not to
construct the desired minor.

The unrooted argument must also handle five-cuts with a side of density
one, where the new rooted theorem does not apply. In a minimum
counterexample, we show that the low-density sides of five-cuts are
consistent. Local contractions and rooted constructions then exclude
vertices of degree five, and an inclusion-minimal fragment excludes all
five-cuts. The resulting six-connectivity allows contractions that force dense neighbourhoods
at vertices of degree six or seven. These give the required minor,
using an adaptation of Norin--Totschnig's degree-six argument and the
finite lemma for degree seven.

We state the external tools precisely in Section~\ref{sec:preliminaries}
and supply the supporting constructions and the full global argument
in the subsequent sections. The only computational input is
Lemma~\ref{lem:quotient}; Appendix~\ref{sec:finite} explains its finite
scope, certificates and reproducibility.
