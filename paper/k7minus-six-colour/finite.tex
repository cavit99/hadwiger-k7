\section{The degree-seven quotient lemma}\label{sec:finite}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:quotient}
Let $R$ be a graph on seven vertices with minimum degree at least four.
Add a vertex $v$ adjacent to every vertex of $R$, and another vertex
$c$ adjacent to at least six vertices of $R$. No edge $vc$ is required.
The resulting graph contains a $K_7^{-}$ minor.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Delete edges if necessary so that $vc$ is absent and $c$ misses exactly
one vertex $x$ of $R$. The complement $F$ of $R$ has maximum degree at
most two. Add edges to $F$ until it is edge-maximal subject to this bound,
obtaining $F'$. Replacing $R$ by $\overline{F'}$ only deletes edges, so
it suffices to find the minor in the resulting graph.

Every component of $F'$ is a path or a cycle. A path of order at least
three could be closed into a cycle, and two distinct path components
could be joined at vertices of degree less than two. Maximality therefore
leaves only cycles and at most one additional $K_1$ or $K_2$. Since $F'$
has seven vertices, its possible forms are
\[
C_7,\quad C_4\dot\cup C_3,\quad C_6\dot\cup K_1,\quad
C_3\dot\cup C_3\dot\cup K_1,\quad C_5\dot\cup K_2.
\]

Label each cycle consecutively as indicated below. Up to automorphisms
of $F'$, there are nine choices of the marked vertex $x$. The table
gives a model for each; two rows each cover two choices. Concatenated
labels denote a single bag, for example $03=\{0,3\}$ and
$2v=\{2,v\}$.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
F'&x&\text{seven branch sets}&\text{only missing pair}\\
\hline
C_7(0,\ldots,6)&0&03,15,2,4,6,v,c&v:c\\
C_4(0,\ldots,3)\dot\cup C_3(4,5,6)&0,4
 &04,1,25,3,6,v,c&v:c\\
C_6(0,\ldots,5)\dot\cup K_1(6)&0
 &024,1,3,5,6,v,c&v:c\\
C_6(0,\ldots,5)\dot\cup K_1(6)&6
 &02,1,3,4c,5,6,v&02:1\\
C_3(0,1,2)\dot\cup C_3(3,4,5)\dot\cup K_1(6)&0
 &03,14,2,5,6,v,c&v:c\\
C_3(0,1,2)\dot\cup C_3(3,4,5)\dot\cup K_1(6)&6
 &03,1c,2,4,5,6,v&4:5\\
C_5(0,\ldots,4)\dot\cup K_2(5,6)&0,5
 &05,1,2v,3,4,6,c&3:4
\end{array}
\]
In every row the bags are disjoint and connected, and all pairs except
the indicated pair are adjacent. For example, in the first row $03$
and $15$ are edges of $\overline{F'}$; together with the singleton bags
$2,4,6$, they are pairwise adjacent. Both $v$ and $c$ are adjacent to
all five bags. The other rows are checked in the same way.
Contracting the bags gives a $K_7^{-}$ minor.
\end{proof}
