# Even-subdivision precursor

This manuscript is a preserved precursor to
[universal bipartite contractibility](../bipartite-contractibility/main.tex).
Its conclusions are subsumed by that theorem. It remains useful for its
direct even-subdivision proof and the degree-three argument retaining
all roots on the opposite shore.

The [source](main.tex), [PDF](main.pdf) and [original internal audit](main_audit.md)
are unchanged. Their original DRAFT and specialist-review wording describes
the historical revision; it does not make this a separate current
publication candidate. The complete pre-organisation state is preserved
at Git revision `f7b52aff0dfb2578bc30ade68f75df12671cf966`.
