# Eight-root absorption at a dominated centre

The roots are the seven common neighbours `Q` and one of the four other
independent degree-eight centres.  The audited eight-terminal theorem gives
a rooted `C8`, `K3,5` or `F8` carrier.  The exceptional-centre bag is then
absorbed into one adjacent `Q`-rooted bag, leaving seven bags, each meeting
`Q`.

The verifier checks every one of the 5,936 labelled carrier masks against
each of the three surviving common-neighbour graphs, choosing the absorbing
owner only after the carrier is known.  A `K5`-minus minor in the resulting
seven-bag quotient lifts to `H`; adjoining the singleton bags `{u},{v}`
gives `K7`-minus in `G`.
